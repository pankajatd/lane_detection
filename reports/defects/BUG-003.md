## 1. Defect Description
High-frequency visual jitter and metric oscillation during continuous video playback. The green lane carpet overlay on the screen was violently shaking, vibrating, and twitching 30 times per second between consecutive frames. Furthermore, the curvature radius display swung wildly (e.g., oscillating between $1,800\text{ m}$ down to $450\text{ m}$ in 0.1 seconds), resulting in an unacceptable, unstable driver console experience.

## 2. Steps to Reproduce
1. In the Streamlit dashboard, select the video playback mode ("Continuous Highway Stream").
2. Click "Start Lane Tracking Stream".
3. Observe the rendered green HUD road carpet and the top KPI metric card.
4. Notice that the green lane carpet vibrates and jerks horizontally on every frame, and the curvature radius numbers jump erratically.

## 3. Expected vs. Actual Behavior
- **Expected:** ADAS augmented-reality green road carpet and vehicle telemetry metrics must remain rock-solid, calm, and smooth across sequential video frames without visual jitter.
- **Actual:** High-frequency jitter caused the lane overlay to vibrate and flicker like a loose light bulb, and curvature numbers oscillated erratically.

## 4. Root Cause Analysis
Video frames were processed in complete temporal isolation with zero memory of prior timestamps. Normal asphalt grain, tire vibrations, and road texture shifts caused minor pixel variations between consecutive photos (30 FPS). Without a temporal smoothing filter, the computer recalculated raw polynomial curves from scratch on every photo, causing the line coordinates to twitch rapidly.

## 5. Developer Resolution (Applied by Software Developer Agent)
- Implemented an **Exponential Moving Average (EMA) Temporal Smoothing Engine** in `src/detector.py`:
  - Blends each new frame's polynomial line with the previous stable frame's line ($75\%\text{ history} + 25\%\text{ new}$):
    $$\text{poly}_{\text{smoothed}} = \alpha \cdot \text{poly}_{\text{current}} + (1 - \alpha) \cdot \text{poly}_{\text{prev}}$$
  - Implemented a 5-frame moving window buffer for curvature radius and lateral offset to filter out single-frame road bumps.
  - Implemented `detector.reset()` lifecycle to cleanly flush smoothing memory buffers when switching driving videos.
