## 1. Defect Description
Under highway road conditions with strong vertical tree shadows or overhead bridge overpasses, high-contrast dark-to-light shadow edges produced steep gradient responses in standard Sobel/Canny edge filtering. The sliding window algorithm latched onto the tree shadow boundary as if it were a painted highway lane line, skewing the ego-vehicle lateral offset to $> +0.85\text{ m}$ and triggering false Lane Departure Warnings on straight, centered driving.

## 2. Steps to Reproduce
1. Ingest highway image containing overhead bridge or tree shadow lines across the driving surface.
2. Observe candidate extraction output in `extract_lane_candidates()`.
3. Vertical shadow edges produce high Sobel-X magnitude, dominating over real painted road markings.
4. Ego-vehicle lateral offset reports `offset = +0.88m` with flashing `WARNING: LANE DEPARTURE RIGHT` on a centered vehicle.

## 3. Expected vs. Actual Behavior
- **Expected:** The vision pipeline must discriminate real painted road markings (white/yellow) from environmental shadows, keeping lateral offset within centered bounds ($\pm 0.30\text{ m}$).
- **Actual:** Shadow lines were falsely classified as lane boundaries, triggering nuisance departure alarms.

## 4. Root Cause Analysis
The candidate extraction pipeline converted RGB directly to grayscale prior to Sobel edge filtering. Grayscale transformation collapses color differences, making high-contrast shadow boundaries indistinguishable from painted lines based on luminance gradient alone.

## 5. Developer Resolution (Applied by Software Developer Agent)
- Upgraded the feature extraction pipeline in `extract_lane_candidates()` to use **HLS (Hue, Lightness, Saturation)** multi-channel thresholding:
  - Isolated the **S (Saturation) channel** (`threshold: 120-255`) to detect yellow lane lines independently of ambient lighting and shadow darkness.
  - Isolated the **L (Lightness) channel** (`threshold: 200-255`) to detect white lane lines while ignoring dark shadow regions.
  - Combined color threshold masks with directional Sobel-X filtering to strictly validate painted line orientation.
